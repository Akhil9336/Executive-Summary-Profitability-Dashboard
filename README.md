# Executive Summary Profitability Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Executive Summary Profitability Dashboard** is an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze business performance, revenue, profitability, sales volume, and order trends. It transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Monitor total revenue and gross profit.
- Analyze gross profit margin and cost of goods sold (COGS).
- Track monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Compare sales performance across countries.
- Understand sales distribution by sales channel and region.
- Present key business metrics in a clear, executive-friendly dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Sales Analysis
Visualizes monthly trends in total revenue and gross profit to identify performance changes throughout the year.

### 2. Country-wise Performance
Compares sales performance across countries, helping identify strong markets and opportunities for improvement.

### 3. Sales Channel Analysis
Shows the distribution of sales across different channels, including:
- Corporate
- Marketplace
- Online Store
- Retail Partners

### 4. Regional Sales Distribution
Provides a regional breakdown of sales across:
- Central Europe
- Northern Europe
- Southern Europe
- Western Europe

### 5. Executive KPI Cards
Displays essential business metrics in a single view for quick performance assessment.

1. Download or clone this repository.

3. Navigate to the dashboard worksheet.
4. Review the KPIs, charts, and sales distributions.
5. Update the source data as needed and refresh the relevant Pivot Tables or charts.
